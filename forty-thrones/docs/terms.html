How prices and tiles work

To take a tile you pay at least 10% more than the last price paid for it. The first purchase of a tile uses the floor price shown on the site. After each purchase the tile is locked for [FREEZE HOURS] hours. The same number is shown on the payment form. When the lock ends, anyone can take the tile by paying at least 10% more.
