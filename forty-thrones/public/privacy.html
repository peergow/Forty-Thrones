What we collect

  • Your Google account email and an internal Google ID, to sign you in.
  • Your public username, and your purchases (tile, price, time).
  • For each purchase, a record of your consent: time, account, terms version, and a one-way hash of your IP address.
  • Reports you send about content.

Payments are handled by our payment provider. We do not receive or store your card details.

What is public

Your username, the prices you paid, and how long you held each tile are shown publicly in tile histories and rankings. The image and caption are public while they are on the board.

Deleting your account

When you delete your account we erase your email and sign-in ID. The public history is kept, because it is the permanent record of the board, but your username is replaced by an anonymous one such as "anon-1a2b3c4d". [Confirm this approach for your jurisdictions.]
